BazaraClip – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

BazaraClip is a personal tool that runs locally on its owner's computer. It turns the owner's own Twitch stream highlights into short videos and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

2. Data accessed

  • TikTok: basic account information (open ID, display name) and permission to upload videos, obtained through TikTok Login Kit with the owner's consent.
  • Instagram / Meta: the owner's Instagram professional account ID and permission to publish Reels.
  • Twitch: public chat messages from the owner's channel, read in real time to detect highlights. Chat messages are stored locally only, to find highlights, and are never shared or sold.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens and all data are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than the official Twitch, TikTok and Meta APIs, plus temporary video hosting used only to deliver a video file to Instagram, deleted right after publishing.

4. Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

5. Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok, Instagram or Twitch app settings. Local data can be deleted by removing the application folder. To request deletion, contact the address below.
